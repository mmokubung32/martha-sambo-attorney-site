Practice Areas

Five areas of law. One consistent standard of care.

Martha represents and advises clients across civil, commercial, labour, criminal and family law — bringing the same rigour and clarity to every matter, regardless of size.

01

Civil Litigation

Martha represents clients in civil disputes from the drafting of summonses and pleadings through to court proceedings and resolution. Her work spans insurance claims matters — including assessment, correspondence and litigation-related work — alongside broader civil disputes requiring careful legal research and strategy.

  • — Summonses, pleadings, motions and heads of argument
  • — Insurance claims: assessment, correspondence and litigation
  • — Court preparation, consultation and representation

02

Commercial Law & Litigation

From contract drafting and review to commercial disputes, Martha supports businesses and institutions that need legal work done properly the first time. This includes experience assisting with the Land Bank restructuring process — reviewing documentation, supporting legal compliance requirements and assisting with institutional legal matters.

  • — Contract drafting and review
  • — Commercial litigation and dispute resolution
  • — Institutional and compliance-related legal support

03

Labour & Employment Law

Martha advises on employment disputes and represents clients through negotiation and alternative dispute resolution processes, working towards cost-effective, practical outcomes rather than protracted conflict wherever possible.

  • — Employment disputes and workplace matters
  • — Negotiation and settlement
  • — Alternative dispute resolution

04

Criminal Law

Martha has assisted clients through criminal law matters, including drafting affidavits and related litigation documents, conducting legal research, and attending court proceedings and consultations, with attention to both process and the person involved.

  • — Affidavits and litigation documents
  • — Court attendance and client consultation
  • — Legal research in support of criminal matters

05

Family Law

Family matters call for legal precision and a steady, human touch. Martha represents clients in family law matters with the same care and confidentiality she brings to every case, mindful that these matters are rarely just legal ones.

  • — Family law advisory and representation
  • — Confidential, client-centred handling of sensitive matters
  • — Negotiated resolution where appropriate
