About Martha

Grounded in the law, guided by people.

Admitted Attorney
High Court of S.A.

Martha Y. Sambo is an admitted Attorney of the High Court of South Africa with over four years of legal experience, including post-admission practice providing legal advice, conducting legal research, drafting legal opinions, contracts and litigation documents, and representing clients in civil, commercial, labour, criminal and family law matters.

She advises clients on legal risk, interprets legislation and case law, negotiates settlements, and manages matters from first instruction through to finalisation. She is currently pursuing an LLM in Banking Law through the University of South Africa, building on an LLB earned at the University of Johannesburg in 2021.

Martha is based in Nelspruit, Mpumalanga, holds a valid Code B driver's licence, and is willing to travel or relocate anywhere in South Africa for the right matter.

Before the Bar

She taught before she practised — and it shows.

In 2017, Martha worked as a student teacher at Sophungane Combined School, facilitating classes and supporting content development and assessment. In 2021, she tutored mathematics at Sikhonza Tutoring Academy, setting and marking mock tests and preparing learners for their exams.

That early instinct — to take something complicated and make it make sense to the person in front of you — carried directly into her legal career. It's part of why she now mentors Candidate Attorneys and contributes to community legal outreach, and why clients tend to describe her advice as clear rather than clever.

Values & Approach

What clients and colleagues can expect

Sound legal judgment

Advice that is well-reasoned and tested against legislation and case law, not just precedent for its own sake.

Attention to detail

Every affidavit, opinion and contract is checked line by line before it reaches a court or counterparty.

Confidentiality
